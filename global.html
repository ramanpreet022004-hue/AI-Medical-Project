AI Around the World

Many countries are investing in Artificial Intelligence to improve healthcare. AI helps doctors detect diseases faster, improve diagnosis, reduce human errors, and provide better treatment for patients.

Major Applications of AI

  • Medical Image Analysis
  • Robot-Assisted Surgery
  • Disease Prediction
  • Virtual Health Assistants
  • Drug Discovery
  • Patient Monitoring

Top Countries Using AI in Healthcare

Country Main AI Applications
United States Medical Imaging, Robotics
Canada Patient Monitoring, AI Research
United Kingdom Digital Healthcare Systems
India Telemedicine and Disease Detection
Japan Healthcare Robots and Elderly Care

Advantages of AI

  1. Faster diagnosis
  2. Accurate treatment
  3. Reduced healthcare costs
  4. Better patient care
  5. Supports doctors in decision making
