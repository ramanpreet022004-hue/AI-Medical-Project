What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming healthcare around the world. Doctors use AI to diagnose diseases, analyze medical images, improve patient care, and perform robotic surgeries.

Benefits of AI in Healthcare

  • Early disease detection
  • Faster diagnosis
  • Robotic surgery
  • Better patient care
  • Reduced medical errors
